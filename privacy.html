Skitter: Games for Cats

Privacy policy

Effective October 2026

Skitter is a game for cats. It works offline, has no accounts and no ads, and doesn't collect personal information about you. This page explains the small amount of data the app does handle.

Data that stays on your device

Your cats' names, play sessions, catch counts and settings are stored only on your phone or tablet. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Anonymous usage and crash data

To improve the app, Skitter uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect:

  • How the app is used, such as which toy was played, session length, and whether a session ended on its timer
  • Device type, operating system version, app version, and approximate country (derived from your network address, which is not stored)
  • Crash reports and technical diagnostics

This data uses a random app-instance identifier. It isn't linked to your name, email or any account. We don't use advertising identifiers, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and the cat names you enter are never sent. You can turn off usage data at any time in Settings, under "Share anonymous usage data".

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. To check whether Skitter Pro is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from the store. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data for advertising.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos.

Camera (future feature)

A future version may let you record your cat while it plays. Recording will only start when you choose to, and clips stay on your device unless you share them.

Children

Skitter is meant for adult cat owners and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Data retention and your choices

Anonymous analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. You can turn off usage data in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. To ask about data linked to your app instance, contact us using the details below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
