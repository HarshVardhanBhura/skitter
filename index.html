Skitter: Games for Cats

Support

Skitter turns your phone or tablet into a hunting game for your cat. Lay the device flat on the floor, pick a toy, and step back.

Common questions

How do I exit a game?

Press and hold the small paw in the corner for 2 seconds, until the ring fills. Then tap the check button that appears. Cats can't do both steps, so they stay in the game.

My cat keeps leaving the app on iPhone or iPad

Turn on Guided Access: open Settings, then Accessibility, then Guided Access, and switch it on. In Skitter, triple-click the side or top button to lock the screen to the game. Triple-click again to unlock.

My cat keeps leaving the app on Android

Use Cat Lock in Skitter's play settings. Android will ask you to pin the app. If pinning isn't available, open Settings, then Security, then App pinning, and switch it on.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Settings in Skitter and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought Skitter Pro with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, then your profile, then Payments and subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn't cancel the subscription.
